# Suggested reviewers: areas only (for the author to confirm)

No names are given: none could be verified as current public experts from this environment. Please choose names yourself
and check that none has a conflict of interest. Suggested areas to search:

1. Urban freight and city logistics policy, access charges and zone-based pricing.
2. Road and congestion pricing, second-best tolls, adaptive (trial-and-error) tolls, distributional effects of charging.
3. Congestion games, Wardrop equilibrium, price of anarchy and convergence of atomic to nonatomic games.
4. Cournot oligopoly with congestion or entry; taxation under imperfect competition and free entry.
5. Mean-field games and finite-population (N-player) approximation, especially finite-state or discrete-time games.
6. Transport externalities: through-traffic delay, emissions and noise valuation for freight vehicles.

Editors may also be asked for people to exclude; none are proposed here.
