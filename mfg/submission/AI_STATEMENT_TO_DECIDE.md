# Generative AI declaration: for the author to decide

Elsevier policy (as given in the Guide for Authors pasted by the author, read 2026-11-04): if generative AI or
AI-assisted technologies were used in the preparation of the manuscript, a statement must be included in a new section
placed before the reference list. Nothing has been written into the manuscript, the cover letter or any other file.

Template wording of the guide (fill the brackets only if the author decides a statement applies):

> Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process
>
> During the preparation of this work the author used [NAME OF TOOL / SERVICE] in order to [REASON]. After using this tool/service, the author reviewed and edited the content as needed and takes full responsibility for the content of the publication.
