# Suggested reviewer areas

Editorial Manager asks for names. These are the areas to search for names (recent authors in Operations Research, Transportation Science, EJOR, Omega, Transportation Research B/C/E, Mathematics of Operations Research, Games and Economic Behavior). No names are proposed here. Avoid reviewers who are authors of the closest neighbours cited in Section 2, or who are close collaborators of the author, unless the author decides otherwise.

1. **Robust and distributionally robust vehicle routing.** Wasserstein, moment and probability-box ambiguity in routing; chance-constrained and data-driven VRP. Covers BRL's criterion and the out-of-sample comparison (Sections 7.1, 7.2).
2. **Exact methods for routing.** Branch-price-and-cut, resource-constrained shortest paths and label dominance, ng-routes. Covers BRL, completion-window dominance and the scale limits (Sections 4.1, 4.2, 7.1).
3. **Imprecise probability and decisions under ambiguity.** Credal sets, upper previsions, E-admissibility, stop-loss and comonotone bounds. Covers Theorem 1 and the credal part of the theory (Sections 3.2, 5).
4. **Congestion, aggregative and integer-programming games.** Potential games, approximate pure equilibria and Shapley--Folkman arguments, complexity of equilibrium existence. Covers Section 5 and the carriers' game.
5. **Road and delivery pricing and bilevel optimisation.** Toll design, implementing a target flow, inverse optimisation, cutting planes for bilevel and Stackelberg problems. Covers DIP (Section 4.4).
6. **Adaptive pricing and incentive design from observed outcomes.** Trial-and-error tolls, marginal-cost and marginal-contribution tariffs, online and learning-based leader updates. Covers ARP and the marginal tariff (Section 4.5).
7. **Urban logistics and city freight policy.** Last-mile operations, curb and low-emission-zone policy, public--private coordination. Covers the policy reading in Section 8 and the instance design in Section 6.
8. **Competition in operations and platform operations.** Cournot-type and capacity-competition models, platform and multi-carrier competition. Covers the carriers' market model (Section 3) and the price-of-anarchy results (Section 7.4).
